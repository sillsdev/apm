# Workflow step assignments

APM evolved from SIL Transcriber, which assigned passages to transcribers and editors only. Assignments now grant **workflow steps** to users or groups — defining who may perform each step on a section or passage.

Sheet structure editing is a separate permission: who may edit the Sections & Passages sheet is assigned to a user or group independently of workflow step assignments.

The Assignments tab and transcriber activity states (Transcribing, Reviewing, Ready to Sync) coexist: step assignment is the general model; activity states remain meaningful for transcription workflow steps.
