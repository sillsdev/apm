- [ ] **[TT-7615](https://jira.sil.org/browse/TT-7615)** - Auto Transcribe does not work correctly when the Transcribe pane contains saved verse markings. [In Progress]

  **Description:**
  *Steps to reproduce:*
  1) Goto the Transcribe pane for Luke passage 1:5-7.
  The passage contains 3 segments with saved verse markings, created using the Mark Verses step.
  
  Segment 1:
  2) Select Segment 1 and click Auto Transcription button.
  Segment 1 is transcribed successfully.
  
  Segment 2:
  3) Click Next Segment button.
  Segment 2 starts playing.
  
  4) Pause Segment 2 and click Auto Transcription button.
  *Problem 1:*
  Segment 1 is transcribed again instead of Segment 2.
  
  Segment 3:
  5) Click Next Segment button.
  Segment 3 starts playing.
  
  5) Pause Segment 3 and click Auto Transcription button.
  *Problem 2:*
  Auto Transcribe does not transcribe the segment.
  So, Auto Transcribe transcribed the same segment twice. In some cases, it does not transcribe the selected segment at all.
  
  *Note:*
  This issue occurs when saved verse markings are present. Auto Transcribe works correctly when there are no saved verse markings.
